Institutional Profile

About Empire Skills India

Read More →

Empire Skills India is an ISO 9001:2015 certified national skill development and vocational training institution committed to bridging the gap between formal education and industry employability. Through our structured curriculum, qualified faculty, and digital academic governance, we empower learners across urban and rural India with verified, career-ready credentials.

Director's Message

“True national progress is achieved when every youth possesses practical, verifiable skills that translate into dignified livelihoods. At Empire Skills India, we uphold academic rigor, transparency in assessment, and lifelong career support for every student who enrolls with us.”

— Office of the Director, Empire Skills India
